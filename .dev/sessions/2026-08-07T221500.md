## Home, reordered

To the proposal's sequence: hero, proof band, pillars, what Overture is made of, featured deployments, three doors, funders and publication. Mostly this was subtraction, and each cut is argued in the route file's comment:

- **The carousel** hid four of five deployments behind arrows. Three featured cards linking to `/impact/<platform>/` say more and hide nothing. Which three is `featured: true` in `platforms.ts`, so changing the pick is a data edit. `[NEEDS: confirmation]` on the current pick, which is the two with verified figures plus the oldest.
- **The role taxonomy** ("a team of software engineers, data scientists and cloud infrastructure specialists") is what the proposal rules against. Attribution is in the hero and the story is on `/about-us/`.
- **The five-component catalogue** listed five of seven with links into `/products/` fragments. Orientation belongs on home, the catalogue belongs on its own page, so it is now the Collect / Explore / Control shape in words and one link.
- **The Prelude walkthrough and terminal.** A second copy of the quickstart, which is finding 5 and the same reasoning that retired `/getting-started/`. "Deploy it" is a door now and points at the docs.
- **"Build. Deploy. Discover."** in both places it appeared.

The hero heading is new: "Open-source building blocks for research data platforms", with the LOI's opening sentence under it. **This is a copy change and wants a read** rather than a decision; it is the one thing in phase 4 that is not mechanical.

**Two findings.** The longer heading runs to three lines at 1440 and the hero was `height: 470px`, a fixed box tuned to a one-line heading. The flex child overflowed in both directions and put the first line behind the navbar: measured a 614px section inside a 470px box, starting 7px above the viewport. `min-height` in `_hero.scss` fixes it for every page and changes none of them, since the other five heroes are shorter than 470px and still end exactly where they did.

And the `ow:md:pb-40` added to the pillars band this morning, to clear a floating cube, is gone: the reorder removed all six cubes, so the padding was clearing nothing. That is the second time in one day a measurement stopped being true because the thing it measured moved.

## /case-studies/ retired

Once home stopped linking into its fragments and the footer stopped listing it, nothing pointed at it, so phase 4 finished the job: the route, `CaseStudiesNav`, and `pages/_case-studies.scss` are gone. The `.Case-Study` block moved to `components/_casestudy.scss`, where the four platform pages that still render it can find it. Its rules were already top-level rather than page-scoped, so nothing changed but the file.

`caseStudyAnchors` and `productsAnchors` are both gone from `constants/pages.ts`, with a note in their place. They existed for home's links into other pages' fragments and nothing links that way now. **Removing them is what cleared the last of the six broken-anchor warnings: `npm run build` is clean for the first time in the port.**
