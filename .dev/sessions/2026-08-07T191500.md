## Decisions worth recording

- **PCGL's launch date is withheld, and the others are not.** The reference material gives OHCRN and the Drug Discovery Portal as 2025 and PCGL as November 2026. Clearance covers all three, so the two past dates are published and are in `metrics.ts`. PCGL's is in the future and is another institution's to announce, so its card reads "In development" and `metrics.ts` has no entry rather than an unused one. `roadmap.md` § Inputs needed now asks only about that one date.
- **Component names on the deployment pages follow the house style.** "File Manager (Song)", "Search (Arranger)" and so on, per the Decided section, which applies to any page describing which parts a platform uses. This also affects `/case-studies/` while it lives, which is an improvement there too.
- **The ICGC-ARGO Ego line is gone without naming a replacement.** It now reads "Access and authorization: stateless authentication, applied across the stack", which is accurate, matches the Control framing on `/products/`, and does not guess. Still worth asking someone who knows.
- **`Check it out!` became `Visit <platform>`.** Inherited Gatsby copy; a button on an evaluator-facing page should say where it goes.
